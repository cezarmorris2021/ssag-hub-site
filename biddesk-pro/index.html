Important distinctions

Clear scope. No fake automation.

Is the quote builder usable?

Yes. The browser app calculates totals, stores quotes locally, produces print-ready proposals and prepares email drafts.

Does it synchronize between devices?

Not in the local edition. Use encrypted device storage and export backups; cloud account access requires a separate scoped implementation.

Can it charge customers?

It can display a business's existing PayPal payment URL in a proposal. The app does not process payments, verify payment status or create invoices.

Can it send automated follow-ups?

It prepares follow-up messages for your email app. Sending and delivery must be confirmed by a real provider before recording a contact.
