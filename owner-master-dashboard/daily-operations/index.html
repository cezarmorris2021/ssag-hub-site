05 • Caller kit

One script, two business-specific endings

Local website introduction

Hello, my name is [your first name] with SSAG. I work with local businesses on websites and customer follow-up. Have I reached the person who handles new customer inquiries or marketing? I noticed an opportunity worth verifying and wanted to see whether a quick preview would be useful. Is there a better time for a two-minute conversation?

Sentinel Zero introduction

Hello, my name is [your first name] with Sentinel Zero. We help businesses review cybersecurity exposure and prioritize practical risk reduction. Who is responsible for cybersecurity or business risk there? If useful, I'd like to offer a brief introductory review conversation. What is the best way to reach that person?

Respect opt-outs and applicable telemarketing, privacy, consent and Do Not Call rules. Don't imply that a vulnerability or missing website has been confirmed without verification.
