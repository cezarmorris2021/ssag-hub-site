# DROP DISTRICT — Creator acquisition kit
## Market
Independent hip-hop / R&B creators, DJs, sneaker and streetwear launches, nightlife teams, tattoo and barber brands, fashion pop-ups. Do not send mass unsolicited messages.
## Offer
First Drop $149: 3 digital promo graphics, 1 release flyer, square/story exports, 1 revision.
Release Mode $349: 8 visuals, 2 short edits based on client footage, captions/posting sequence, 2 revisions.
Full Takeover $699: 16 visuals, 4 edits, one-sheet/press kit, 14-day posting calendar, 2 revisions.
## Outreach (permission-first)
Hey, I saw your latest drop. I run Drop District, a creator-focused content studio. We make original rollout visuals, reels and launch kits built around your look. Want the price menu? No pressure.
## Objection handling
Already have a designer: Understood — we can help with overflow during a launch if you're interested.
Price: The entry package is $149 with defined assets and one revision; custom changes are quoted separately.
Timing: We only commit to delivery after reviewing footage, availability and final scope.
## Close
Ask which package fits. Invite the client to the public intake page. After submission, review brief, confirm material rights and delivery plan in writing; send PayPal invoice; begin after verified payment.
## Fulfillment
Validate releases/music/image rights; collect licensed assets via approved method; write creative brief; generate only original/licensed assets; perform QC on titles, copy, dimensions, export codecs, spelling; deliver files with inventory; document delivery and revisions.
## Channels
Creator DMs (only when welcome), artist referrals, fashion pop-ups, community events, barber networks, independent label partnerships, campus creator groups and event organizers.
## 7-day marketing rhythm
Day 1 publish three original style studies clearly marked concepts. Day 2 invite opt-in creator partners. Day 3 share process and before/after composition breakdowns without fabricated client results. Day 4 request referrals. Day 5 follow up only on consented inquiries. Day 6 post service deliverables and transparent package prices. Day 7 review actual inquiries, responses and invoices; never claim results without evidence.
