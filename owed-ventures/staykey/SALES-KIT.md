# STAYKEY — Host acquisition kit
## Market
Independent short-term rental operators, vacation-rental hosts, property managers and boutique lodging owners.
## Offer
Welcome Essential $179: mobile guide, check-in/out sections, house rules/contact, 1 revision.
Signature Stay $399: Essential plus QR signage files, host-approved local recommendations, pre-arrival and FAQ templates, 2 revisions.
Host Portfolio $749: up to 3 guides, branded QR files, unified messaging templates, documented owner handoff, 2 revisions.
## Outreach
Hi, I help hosts turn scattered check-in instructions into a polished guest guide guests can open from a QR code. Could I send the one-page scope and pricing?
## Fulfillment
Confirm host authority; collect approved, non-sensitive property content; no account credentials or door/access codes in public intake; verify all information with host; create mobile guide and QR materials; test links on Android and iPhone; deliver files, revision instructions and support boundaries.
## Acquisition
Host groups (only where self-promotion is permitted), local property managers, housekeeping vendors serving STRs, independent co-host partnerships, opt-in referrals and owner/operator events.
## Payment
Review request, confirm scope, send PayPal Business invoice manually, verify payment in PayPal before work, update owner status.
## Policies
Don't publish personal addresses, security system configurations or access codes without the owner's explicit written instructions and suitable access controls. Don't make booking-rate claims without measured evidence.
