Security and completeness statement

This version is a fully functional single-browser sales workspace. It has no cloud CRM synchronization, shared employee access, automatic texting, call recording, financial verification, or server-side authentication. Browser storage can be erased. Do not enter sensitive identity documents, passwords or financial credentials. Use JSON backups regularly.

Evidence fields and DNC switches assist internal process discipline; they do not on their own establish regulatory compliance. Follow applicable telemarketing, consent, and privacy requirements.
