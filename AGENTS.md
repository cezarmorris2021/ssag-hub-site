# Repository work rules

These rules apply to this repository. Keep its existing purpose clear and do not add features that the owner did not request.

## How to explain the work
- Use everyday words and short steps. Explain technical terms the first time they appear. The owner is not expected to know coding terms.
- Say what changed, where information is stored, what is connected, what has actually been checked, and what still cannot be confirmed.
- Do not say a feature is working or finished without evidence from the running version.

## Real data and honest claims
- Never invent customers, leads, calls, appointments, sales, revenue, approvals, integrations, or test results.
- Keep clearly labeled sample records separate from real business records and exclude samples from totals and reports.
- A deployment marked “Ready” only means the host accepted a build. It does not prove the page is reachable, features work, data saves, or a real transaction completed.
- Client-facing pages must not expose owner notes or internal instructions, use unsupported claims, or imply a concept preview is approved.

## Data, access, and owner control
- Explain whether information stays in this browser/device or saves to a shared service. Browser-only storage does not sync to a team and is not a verified CRM.
- Do not put passwords, payment details, private customer information, or service keys in public page code or browser storage.
- Protect owner and customer records with appropriate sign-in and server-side access checks. Keep suppression and do-not-contact rules intact.
- Preserve existing records. Do not reset, delete, expose, or migrate user data without a safe backup and clear authorization.

## Build quality
- Make the experience usable on phones and accessible with clear labels, readable text, and visible action results.
- Every form and button must do what its label says. Be clear when a feature is unavailable or still needs a real service connection.
- For business records, capture the owner, date/time when relevant, status, and next action.
- Keep a short record of changes and the evidence used to confirm them. List any blocker and the smallest practical next step.
